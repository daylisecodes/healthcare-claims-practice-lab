Privacy

Privacy-first by keeping the demo synthetic.

The current version does not require accounts, uploads, bank or insurance connections, patient portals, or claim submission.

Information this demo is designed to use

Only fictional training scenarios built into the website. The practice module uses selections you make in your browser and does not ask you to type patient names, member IDs, medical details, or other sensitive information.

Do not enter PHI

Do not use real patient information, protected health information (PHI), insurance identifiers, Social Security numbers, real claim data, or employer-confidential records in this public demo.

Browser processing

The current practice checks and session progress indicator run in the browser. No account or database is required. Practice progress is not designed to be permanently stored and resets when the page is refreshed.

Hosting analytics

If privacy-friendly hosting analytics are enabled on the public deployment, they may provide aggregate information such as page views, device type, or referring site. The project will not intentionally use analytics to collect claim content or patient information.
