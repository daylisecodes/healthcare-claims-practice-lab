# Healthcare Claims Practice Lab

Healthcare Claims Practice Lab is a free public educational healthcare technology demo using synthetic training scenarios only. It was created for portfolio and training purposes and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any commercial healthcare or claims entity.

Live demo: https://healthcare-claims-practice-lab.vercel.app/

Anyone may use the live demo to learn from the synthetic scenarios. The source code may be publicly viewable for employer and portfolio review, but this project is **not open-source**. Copying, modifying, republishing, redistributing, selling, sublicensing, or creating derivative works from the project code, original design, or original training content is not permitted without written permission.

## Current modules

- Claim Readiness Practice: review fictional administrative scenarios, select what appears complete, and compare your choices with the scenario's educational follow-up items.
- Coverage Review Basics: identify missing or inconsistent information in fictional coverage snapshots without performing real eligibility verification.
- Privacy & Data Awareness: distinguish safe synthetic training content from real patient or insurance identifiers that should not be entered into a public demo.
- Session progress indicator: tracks completed modules only while the page is open and does not require an account or database.

## Safety and privacy boundaries

- No real patient data or PHI.
- No accounts, uploads, databases, or payer connections in the initial version.
- No real claim submission, eligibility verification, coding determination, or medical advice.
- Training service and diagnosis references are fictional and are not billable codes.
- The project does not reproduce CMS-1500, UB-04, CPT descriptions, X12 837 specifications, or proprietary payer forms.
