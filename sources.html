Sources & boundaries

Primary sources used to shape the educational guardrails.

The app uses original training scenarios. These official resources support basic concepts and explain why some proprietary healthcare content is intentionally not reproduced.

  • Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS): National Provider Identifier Standard. Explains that an NPI is a 10-position numeric identifier used in HIPAA administrative and financial transactions. Open CMS source
  • U.S. Department of Health & Human Services (HHS): Guidance on De-identification of Protected Health Information. Explains PHI and the HIPAA Privacy Rule's de-identification approaches. Open HHS source
  • CMS: Healthcare Common Procedure Coding System (HCPCS). Explains the distinction between HCPCS Level I (CPT) and Level II. Open CMS source
  • CMS: AMA CPT license notice. CMS states that CPT codes and descriptions are copyrighted by the American Medical Association and subject to license restrictions. This project therefore does not reproduce CPT content. Open CMS license notice
Source approach: We link to authoritative material rather than copying proprietary forms, code-set descriptions, or payer manuals into the demo.
